Who We Are

Hope Community Church, part of The Apostolic Church network, is led by Pastors Craig and Yvonne. We welcome everyone in Clydebank with open arms.

We believe in meeting people right where they are—whether you're exploring faith, seeking community, or need support through life's challenges. Our doors are open to all, no matter your background or current circumstances.

Our Mission

Hope For Recovery is a dynamic movement where lives are radically transformed through the power of the Gospel. We equip warriors with battle-tested tools through our 12-week Fruit of Freedom course — a scriptural arsenal for lasting victory.

Our program focuses on healing, growth, and building a supportive community where individuals can find hope and restoration.
